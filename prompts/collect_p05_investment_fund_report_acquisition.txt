# TAXMINER — COLLECT P05 — INVESTMENT FUND REPORT ACQUISITION (FII, FIDC, FIP, FIAGRO)
Prompt Pack: 1.0 (addendum, decision_0024) | Method: CO-STAR | Technical naming: en-US | Explanations: pt-BR

## C — CONTEXT
TaxMiner treats CVM investment fund reports as a first-class source family, `investment_fund_reports`, alongside `tax_consultation_decisions` and `financial_disclosures` (MVP Phase 4, `req_0012`). Four subfamilies are in scope: `fii` (real estate), `fidc` (receivables), `fip` (private equity) and `fiagro` (agribusiness). The CVM open-data portal (https://dados.cvm.gov.br/dados/, ODbL, Crawl-Delay 10) publishes them as static yearly or monthly ZIP/CSV files. Listings were verified on 2026-11-10 (`docs/data_sources.md`):
- `FII/DOC/{INF_MENSAL,INF_TRIMESTRAL,INF_ANUAL}`: yearly ZIPs since 2016;
- `FII/DOC/DFIN`: yearly CSV index;
- `FIDC/DOC/INF_MENSAL`: monthly ZIPs, older years under `HIST/`;
- `FIP/DOC/INF_TRIMESTRAL`: yearly CSV 2010–2023;
- `FIP/DOC/INF_QUADRIMESTRAL`: yearly CSV since 2024;
- `FIAGRO/DOC/INF_MENSAL`: monthly ZIPs since 2025-05.
Reuse the shared reliability primitives from `common_p07_new_data_sources.txt` (`src/common/`) once they are implemented.

## O — OBJECTIVE
Discover, retrieve and incrementally refresh the published fund report files for each subfamily, independently of the other source families. Each run must provide:
- reproducible provenance and lawful access;
- immutable originals with per-member SHA-256;
- versioning and coverage metrics;
- resumable execution.

## S — STYLE

Use lowercase American English snake_case for technical names; keep CVM file, table and field names exactly as published. Keep a separate namespace and manifest per subfamily. Do not create empty placeholders or claim unexecuted work.

## R — EXECUTION PLAN
### 00 — Source verification and coverage
Re-verify each subfamily:
- dataset pages and listings, and the `META/` dictionaries;
- license, robots.txt and crawl delay;
- file naming, first and last available periods, and update cadence;
- layout changes over time.
Record verified patterns with `verified_at` in `config/investment_fund_report_sources.yml` and `docs/data_sources.md`. Never invent APIs or claim full history. Stop on 403/429.
### 01 — Source identity and discovery
Define these fields:
- `source_id` (`cvm:{family}:{dataset}:{file_name}`), `family`, `dataset`, `reference_period`;
- `file_name`, `source_url`, `last_modified` and `size` (taken from the listing);
- `file_format` and `download_outcome`.
Account for these cases:
- files republished under the same name: track them by content hash and listing timestamp;
- the FIDC `HIST/` folder;
- the FIP change from quarterly to four-monthly reports.
### 02 — Download and manifest
Implement `src/collect/discover_fund_reports.R` and `src/collect/download_fund_reports.R` with:
- polite throttling (at least 10 s) and timeouts;
- retry/backoff only for timeouts and 5xx, and an immediate stop on 403/429;
- ZIP signature and member checks (count and size limits, unsafe or duplicate names);
- per-member SHA-256 and an append-only manifest.
Keep raw bytes immutable under `TAXMINER_DATA_DIR/raw/investment_fund_reports/{fii,fidc,fip,fiagro}/`, outside the repository and OneDrive.
### 03 — Historical and incremental
Support bounded historical backfills and incremental refresh with checkpoints and idempotent reruns. Re-check the most recent periods on each refresh, because the CVM republishes those files. Do not infer completeness from download counts.
### 04 — Integration and tests
- Keep `tax_consultation_decisions` and `financial_disclosures` acquisition unaffected.
- Do not change `src/collect/` behaviour used by a running collection without authorization.
- Provide synthetic fixtures for listings, republished files, truncated ZIPs, unsafe member names, HTTP errors and resumability.
- Acquire a small verified public sample only after explicit authorization.

## MANDATORY FOUR-PHASE MVP ROADMAP (PROMPT PACK 1.0; PHASE 4 ADDED BY decision_0024)
- **MVP Phase 1 — Tax Consultation Decisions:** independently discover, acquire, process, consolidate in Parquet, and search public RFB Solucoes de Consulta; provide a working local Shiny search, original document references, tests, and explicit human acceptance.
- **MVP Phase 2 — Financial Statement Notes:** independently discover, acquire, process, consolidate in Parquet, and search public explanatory notes from companies' financial statements; provide company/CNPJ/period/note filters, source references, tests, and explicit human acceptance. Phase 2 must be usable without Phase 1 search.
- **MVP Phase 3 — Cross-Source Intelligence:** integrate searches and evidence-based relationships across the two accepted datasets; support side-by-side source citations, confidence/uncertainty, traceability, tests, and explicit human acceptance. No automatic findings of misconduct.
- **MVP Phase 4 — Investment Fund Reports:** independently discover, acquire, consolidate in Parquet, and search public CVM fund reports for FII, FIDC, FIP and FIAGRO (`investment_fund_reports`); provide family/fund CNPJ/administrator/period filters, source-file citations, tests, and explicit human acceptance. Phase 4 must be usable without Phases 1–3; Phase 3 does not consume fund data unless a later decision extends it.
Track implementation, testing, human acceptance, release and operational health separately FOR EACH phase. A phase may start before the previous phase's acceptance only when documented dependencies are satisfied; no phase can be marked accepted without human sign-off. Keep one local Shiny interface with independently enabled functional areas.
Naming: `tax_consultation_decisions` is the technical document family for Solucoes de Consulta; `financial_disclosures`/`financial_notes` for corporate reports and notes; `investment_fund_reports` (subfamilies `fii`, `fidc`, `fip`, `fiagro`) for CVM fund reports; `document_relationships` for cross-source evidence. Preserve the original Portuguese legal title and document type. Do not equate Solucoes de Divergencia, Pareceres Normativos or other administrative acts with Solucoes de Consulta: use separate typed classifications under a broader `tax_interpretive_decisions` umbrella if scope expands. Do not use `tax_rulings`, `ruling_id`, or 'Tax Rulings' in newly authored artifacts.
Protected `restricted/` and `tmp/` stay inaccessible to Codex and other remote agents; local-only independent Git history may be managed by the developer, but must never be synchronized or published.
