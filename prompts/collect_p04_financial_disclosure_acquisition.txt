# TAXMINER — COLLECT P04 — FINANCIAL DISCLOSURE ACQUISITION
Prompt Pack: 1.0 | Method: CO-STAR | Technical naming: en-US | Explanations: pt-BR

## C — CONTEXT
The Collect module is planned to acquire official tax consultation decisions (Phase 1.1). Provide, independently of that pipeline, a separately configured pipeline for publicly disclosed company financial statements and explanatory notes. Sources may include CVM disclosures and company investor-relations portals, subject to verified access and licensing.

## O — OBJECTIVE
Discover, retrieve and incrementally refresh historical public financial reports with reproducible provenance, lawful access, immutable originals, versioning, completeness metrics and resumable execution.

## S — STYLE
Modular, incremental, reproducible, audit-ready R code. Use lowercase American English snake_case for all technical names; retain legally meaningful source-language content. Do not create empty placeholders or claim unexecuted work.

## T — TONE
Precise, skeptical of unverified sources, explicit about gaps and uncertainty.

## A — AUDIENCE
TaxMiner developer, Codex/RStudio contributors, reviewers and authorized approvers.

## R — EXECUTION PLAN
### 00 — Source verification and coverage
Research actual official/public disclosure endpoints and metadata, publication rules, historical coverage, formats (PDF, HTML, XBRL where available), rate limits, robots/access terms and whether report/notes are available. Record verified URL patterns, retrieval date and uncertainties in `docs/data_sources.md` and `config/financial_disclosure_sources.yml`. Never invent APIs or claim full history.
### 01 — Source identity and discovery
Define `source_id`, `company_cnpj` (only if verified), `company_name`, `report_id`, `report_type`, `statement_scope`, `reporting_period_start`, `reporting_period_end`, `filing_date`, `publication_date`, `source_url`, `document_version`, `language`, `file_format`. Account for missing identifiers, name changes, consolidated and individual reports, amendments and duplicate filings.
### 02 — Download and manifest
Implement `src/collect/discover_financial_reports.R`, `src/collect/download_financial_reports.R` and a reusable manifest; use source-specific adapters, polite throttling, timeouts, retry/backoff, conditional requests when supported, content-type checks, checksums, stable IDs and error categorization. Keep raw bytes immutable under `data/raw/financial_disclosures/` (local artifacts only unless explicit publication approval). Store download timestamps and source evidence.
### 03 — Historical and incremental
Support bounded historical backfills and incremental refresh with checkpoints, idempotent reruns, document version tracking and source-specific coverage reports. Do not infer historical completeness from successful download count. Expose CLI entrypoints only when justified.
### 04 — Integration and tests
Keep existing `tax_consultation_decisions` acquisition unaffected. Provide public/synthetic fixtures for pagination, network failures, duplicate/version cases, PDF/HTML formats, CNPJ ambiguity, resumability and corrupted files. Provide a small verified public sample for the prototype and record any access blockers.

## MANDATORY MVP ROADMAP (PROMPT PACK 1.0; RESTRUCTURED BY decision_0025)
- **MVP Phase 1 — Data Collection, Structuring and Search:** three independent subphases, one per source family. Each has its own schema, provenance, coverage, quality, tests, local Shiny search area and explicit human acceptance; none depends on another.
  - **Phase 1.1 — Tax Consultation Decisions:** independently discover, acquire, process, consolidate in Parquet, and search public RFB Solucoes de Consulta; provide a working local Shiny search, original document references, tests, and explicit human acceptance.
  - **Phase 1.2 — Financial Statement Notes:** independently discover, acquire, process, consolidate in Parquet, and search public explanatory notes from companies' financial statements; provide company/CNPJ/period/note filters, source references, tests, and explicit human acceptance.
  - **Phase 1.3 — Investment Fund Reports:** independently discover, acquire, consolidate in Parquet, and search public CVM fund reports for FII, FIDC, FIP and FIAGRO (`investment_fund_reports`); provide family/fund CNPJ/administrator/period filters, source-file citations, tests, and explicit human acceptance.
- **MVP Phase 2 — Cross-Source Intelligence:** evidence-based relationships between the bases accepted in Phase 1, always as cited hypotheses with side-by-side source citations, confidence/uncertainty, traceability, tests and human review. It consumes only subphase datasets that have explicit human acceptance. No automatic findings of misconduct.
Track implementation, testing, human acceptance, release and operational health separately FOR EACH subphase and phase. Subphases 1.1–1.3 run independently; Phase 2 work may start before every subphase is accepted only when its documented dependencies are satisfied; no phase can be marked accepted without human sign-off. Keep one local Shiny interface with independently enabled functional areas.
Naming: `tax_consultation_decisions` is the technical document family for Solucoes de Consulta; `financial_disclosures`/`financial_notes` for corporate reports and notes; `investment_fund_reports` (subfamilies `fii`, `fidc`, `fip`, `fiagro`) for CVM fund reports; `document_relationships` for cross-source evidence. Preserve the original Portuguese legal title and document type. Do not equate Solucoes de Divergencia, Pareceres Normativos or other administrative acts with Solucoes de Consulta: use separate typed classifications under a broader `tax_interpretive_decisions` umbrella if scope expands. Do not use `tax_rulings`, `ruling_id`, or 'Tax Rulings' in newly authored artifacts.
Protected `restricted/` and `tmp/` stay inaccessible to Codex and other remote agents; local-only independent Git history may be managed by the developer, but must never be synchronized or published.
