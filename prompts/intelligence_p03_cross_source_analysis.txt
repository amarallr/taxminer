# TAXMINER — INTELLIGENCE P03 — CROSS-SOURCE ANALYSIS
Prompt Pack: 1.0 | Method: CO-STAR | Technical naming: en-US | Explanations: pt-BR

## C — CONTEXT
TaxMiner Intelligence must jointly research Receita Federal tax consultation decisions and companies’ published financial explanatory notes while preserving the different legal and evidentiary roles of each source.

## O — OBJECTIVE
Build explainable, cited cross-source search and comparison: legal topics, company disclosure trends, source-to-source links and analyst-reviewable hypotheses without automated allegations or unsupported legal conclusions.

## S — STYLE
Modular, incremental, reproducible, audit-ready R code. Use lowercase American English snake_case for all technical names; retain legally meaningful source-language content. Do not create empty placeholders or claim unexecuted work.

## T — TONE
Precise, skeptical of unverified sources, explicit about gaps and uncertainty.

## A — AUDIENCE
TaxMiner developer, Codex/RStudio contributors, reviewers and authorized approvers.

## R — EXECUTION PLAN
### 00 — Evidence model
Model `document_relationships` with `relationship_id`, `source_document_id`, `target_document_id`, `relationship_type`, `tax_topic`, `matching_method`, `evidence_excerpt`, `source_page`, `confidence_score`, `review_status`, `reviewer`, `created_at`. A tax consultation decision is an administrative interpretation; a financial note is a company disclosure. Never infer legal compliance or noncompliance from topical similarity.
### 01 — Unified search
Extend keyword and structured search to both families; filters include `document_family`, `company_cnpj`, `company_name`, `reporting_period`, `report_type`, `statement_scope`, `tax_topic`, `consultation_decision_number`, `consultation_decision_year`, `issuing_authority`. Results must expose original citation, document version, source page and extraction quality; distinguish direct quotes, normalized metadata and AI-generated commentary.
### 02 — Cross-source linking
Use auditable keyword/legal-reference extraction first; optional embeddings/RAG only after offline evaluation. Preserve exact textual evidence, source page, document URLs, retrieval dates and uncertainty. Implement `src/intelligence/link_consultation decisions_to_disclosures.R`, `src/intelligence/compare_company_disclosures.R`, `src/intelligence/analyze_disclosure_trends.R`. No fabricated link, case-law interpretation, CNPJ match, confidence score or source.
### 03 — Comparisons and longitudinal analysis
Compare notes across years, versions and companies, respecting reporting scope, changes in accounting policies, amendments and financial units. Highlight changes in text and disclosed figures as observations, not proof of wrongdoing. Explicitly disclose selection bias and incomplete coverage.
### 04 — Prototype interface
Add local Shiny `Cross-Source Research` view with two source families, company/year/topic filters, side-by-side source excerpts, original links, optional analyst review status, and clear labels for hypothesis vs verified source fact. Keep initial MVP bounded and separately track additional scope approval.
### 05 — Evaluation and safety
Test retrieval precision on public labeled examples, citation/page accuracy, false link rates, missing documents, contradictory disclosures and date/version handling. Never use confidential local data, upload protected documents to AI APIs or imply a tax audit determination.
