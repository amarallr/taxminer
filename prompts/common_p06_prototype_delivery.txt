Preserve original Portuguese text, issuing organization, company, reporting scope, document dates, original units, page/table locations, revisions and source URLs. All NEW technical identifiers MUST be American English (`en-US`) snake_case. Never fabricate access, document counts, CNPJ matches, legal conclusions, citations, source relationships or test results.
For financial disclosures, verify real public sources and access conditions first (e.g. CVM disclosure systems, company investor-relations websites); do not assume API endpoints or historical coverage. Model report -> note -> note section/table, and entity/company -> report, retaining consolidated vs separate statements, annual vs interim periods, comparative figures, units, restatements, filing/publication dates, and version lineage. Match CNPJ only with verified evidence; represent uncertainty explicitly. Any AI-inferred links between consultation decisions and company notes are hypotheses requiring citations and confidence/validation, NEVER findings of tax misconduct.
`.restricted/` and `.tmp/` remain inaccessible to Codex/remote agents, exclusively local and never published or synchronized. Developer-only independent local Git history is permitted without remotes. Authentication only just in time through official local flows, never secrets in prompts.
